## LifeOS Feature Goals

- **Scoring & XP Engine**
  - Quests mapped to real-life habits and tasks.
  - XP accrual, streaks, and level progression.
  - XP sinks for Indulgences (reels, snacks, discretionary time).

- **AI Oracle**
  - RAG over journals, health data, and habit scores.
  - Firm but constructive coaching with concrete next steps.
  - Deep Mode–aware responses that respect privacy state.

- **Deep Mode**
  - Dual-state privacy for sensitive domains (sobriety, mental health, private journals).
  - Explicit Deep Mode toggle, enforced in rendering and data access.
  - Redaction/aggregation of Deep Mode data when in Normal Mode.

- **Memory Lane**
  - MemoryLaneGallery for past highlights, wins, and key reflections.
  - Upload pipeline for images, documents, and artifacts (UploadButton).
  - Tagging and search integrated with Oracle context.

- **Comfort Zone Tasks**
  - Challenges that push social, academic, and physical comfort zones.
  - Structured logging and reflection flows after each challenge.
  - XP and achievements tied to completion and reflection quality.

- **Academic Tracker**
  - Attendance tracking per course.
  - Study sessions, deadlines, and exam preparation quests.
  - Oracle summaries and risk flags based on academic consistency.
